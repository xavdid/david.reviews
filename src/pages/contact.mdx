# Drop me a line

I love to hear from readers! Please drop a line via email [hello @ david.reviews](mailto:hello@david.reviews) with any comments, concerns, recommendations, or bug reports.

You can also mention me on <MastodonIcon />[Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@xavdid) or <BlueskyIcon />[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/david.reviews) if that's more convenient.

## Developers / Publishers

If you'd like me to review your game (whether it's coming soon or already released), please get in touch! Email is best: [games @ david.reviews](mailto:games@david.reviews). I'm also on <DiscordIcon />[Discord](https://discord.com/users/249314141683056640) as `@xavdid`.

The bad news is that I can only cover a fraction of the games I'm contacted about. Declining to review your game isn't a comment on its quality, I just have to be choosy with my time.

> Please note: I don't have the bandwidth to write reviews for demos or early-access releases. The best time to reach out is when your 1.0 is on the horizon.

### The deal

In exchange for being provided a review key, I'm happy to publish an honest review of your game. Depending on my bandwidth, it may be a shortish one (like the one I wrote for <SubtleLink href="/games/dragon-quest-xi-echoes-of-an-elusive-age/#2025-09-14">Dragon Quest XI</SubtleLink>) or a longer feature (like my [Roottrees are Dead](/articles/the-roottrees-are-dead-review/) article). If a shorter review is a dealbreaker, please get in touch before sending a key.

If you send an unsolicited key / game to my Steam curator, I reserve the right to choose the review type based on my availability or _not review your game at all_. I mostly review games I think I'll enjoy, so talking to me first is the best way to ensure coverage.

Lastly, I do my best to accommodate any deadlines you have, but I've got a day job. If you need the review published by a certain date, please include that information in your email and I'll see what I can do!

### What I like

I gravitate towards [platformers](/games/genres/platformer/), [puzzle](/games/genres/puzzle/) games (especially if there's a story), [action](/games/genres/punching/) games, [tactics](/games/genres/tactics/) / [RPGs](/games/genres/turn-based/), and [programming](/games/genres/programming/) games. I'm pretty open minded though, so don't worry if your game doesn't fit neatly into one of my boxes!

### What I don't like

I am unlikely to review fighting games, anything horror-related, PVP games, microtransactions, endless games (e.g. I want a final boss or some exit condition), or anything involving AI art.

### Available Platforms

I'm able to review games on the following platforms (listed in roughly decreasing order of preference):

- Steam (on a Steam Machine / Macbook Pro)
- PS5
- Xbox Series S
- Switch

## Authors
