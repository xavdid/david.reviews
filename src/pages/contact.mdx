# Drop me a line

I love to hear from readers! Please drop a line via email [hello @ david.reviews](mailto:hello@david.reviews) with any comments, concerns, recommendations, or bug reports.

You can also mention me on <MastodonIcon />[Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@xavdid) or <BlueskyIcon />[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/david.reviews) if that's more convenient.

## Developers / Publishers

If you'd like me to review your game (whether it's coming soon or already released), please get in touch! Email is best: [games @ david.reviews](mailto:games@david.reviews). I'm also on <DiscordIcon />[Discord](https://discord.com/users/249314141683056640) as `@xavdid`.

### The deal

I'm always on the hunt for games that my readers and I will enjoy. Unfortunately, I can only cover a fraction of the games I'm contacted about. Declining to review your game isn't a comment on its quality, I just have to be choosy with my time.

The simplest way to get your game in front of me is to **send me a review code and your press kit**. I'll take a look, try it out, and let you know if it's something I can cover on the site.

> Please note: I don't have the bandwidth to write reviews for demos or early-access releases. The best time to reach out is when your 1.0 is on the horizon.

Depending on my bandwidth, it may be a shortish review (like the one I wrote for <SubtleLink href="/games/dragon-quest-xi-echoes-of-an-elusive-age/#2025-09-14">Dragon Quest XI</SubtleLink>) or a longer feature (like my [Galley House](/articles/galley-house-review/) article). I do my best to accommodate any deadlines you have, but I've got a day job. If you need the review published by a certain date, please include that information in your email and I'll see what I can do! And if your other materials don't include it, please mention roughly how long it takes to reach the credits.

Lastly, ❗**do not let an LLM write your pitch email.**❗ I get a million of them, they all read the same, and they all go right in the trash. A form letter you send all publications is fine, but type the original with your actual fingers and brain. Same goes for your Steam capsule image and marketing site; make them yourself. It shows you care about the players' experience.

### Things I like

I gravitate towards [platformers](/games/genres/platformer/), [puzzle](/games/genres/puzzle/) games (especially if there's a story), [action](/games/genres/punching/) games, [tactics](/games/genres/tactics/) / [RPGs](/games/genres/turn-based/), and [programming](/games/genres/programming/) games.

I'm pretty open minded though, so don't worry if your game doesn't fit neatly into one of my boxes!

### Things I don't like

If your game fits into any of these categories, I'm going to pass, so you can save us both the trouble.

- horror / jump scare games
- PVP games
- games with microtransactions
- endless games (i.e. I want a final boss or some exit condition).
- games with AI art _or_ an LLM-generated marketing website. I'm much more interested in a game with "bad" art than one that a model spit out.

### Available platforms

I'm able to review games on the following platforms (listed in roughly decreasing order of preference):

- Steam (on a Steam Machine / Macbook Pro)
- PS5
- Xbox Series S
- Switch

Note that I don't have an Android device, so if your game is Android-only, I am unable to review it.
